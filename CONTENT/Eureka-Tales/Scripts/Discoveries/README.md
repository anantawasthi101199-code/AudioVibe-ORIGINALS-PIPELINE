### Facts: check before publishing

- Snow: 127 deaths in three days, more than 600 overall; brewery about 70 workers; workhouse over 500; the Hampstead widow and niece; parish board 7 September, handle off 8 September; Whitehead and the baby at 40 Broad Street; Koch 1883.
- Pasteur: Lille 1856; swan-neck flasks around 1860; about 55 degrees for wine; silkworms and three daughters' deaths; stroke 1868; chicken cholera 1879; the 1881 anthrax trial; Meister 6 July 1885, fourteen bites, thirteen injections; the shepherd boy October 1885; Geison on the notebooks; Meister as caretaker.
- Galileo: bell tower demonstration August 1609; 7 and 13 January 1610 Jupiter observations; Starry Messenger March 1610; Venus phases late 1610; 1616 warning; Urban VIII 1623; Dialogue 1632; 22 June 1633 abjuration; torture threatened, not used; Maria Celeste took on his penances, died 1634; 1992 statement.
- Franklin: exact date of the kite is unknown; Priestley account. Curie: quantities of ore, notebooks handling rules, one million soldiers estimate.
- Mary Anning: lightning (1800), Joseph's skull 1811 and the body about 1812, sale price about 23 pounds, Birch sale 1820, plesiosaur December 1823 and Cuvier's doubt, pterosaur 1828, Tray 1833, death 1847, Geological Society women from 1919, statue 2022.
- Kepler: the eight-day bet, the two-minute model and the eight-minute gap, egg shape, ellipse 1605, Astronomia Nova 1609, third law 1619, mother's trial 1615-1621, death in Regensburg 1630, epitaph wording (paraphrased).

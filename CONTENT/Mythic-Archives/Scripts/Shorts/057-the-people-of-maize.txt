## story

[warmly] This is the Maya story of how people were made, from the Popol Vuh, the sacred book of the K'iche' Maya of Guatemala. It took the gods four tries.

[softly] In the beginning, there was only the calm sea and the empty sky. The makers, the Heart of Sky and the Plumed Serpent, talked together in the dark, and raised the earth out of the water. They made the mountains and the forests. [thoughtful] But they wanted someone who could speak, who would know them and remember them.

[lightly] First they made the animals. Deer, birds, jaguars, snakes. Speak our names, the gods said. [wry] But the animals could only squawk, howl and hiss. So the gods sent them to live in the forests and canyons, to be hunted and eaten.

[curious] Next they made a person out of mud. [grave] But it was soft and crumbly. It could not turn its head, and its face slumped to one side. It could talk, but made no sense. And when it got wet, it simply melted away.

[thoughtful] So they carved people out of wood. These looked right. They walked and talked, and had children. [darkly] But they had no hearts and no minds. They forgot the gods who made them completely. [intense] So the gods sent a great flood of thick black rain. Even the wooden people's own pots and grinding stones turned against them and attacked them. The few who escaped into the trees became monkeys.

[softly] The gods were in despair. [pause] Then four animals came to them with news.

## ending

[with wonder] A fox, a coyote, a parrot and a crow had found a place full of food, between the mountains. Yellow corn and white corn, maize, ripe and golden. [tender] The grandmother of the gods took the yellow and white maize, and ground it nine times into a fine flour, and mixed it with water. From that dough, the makers shaped the first four men. Their flesh was made of maize.

[delighted] They were perfect. They thanked the gods at once. And they could see everything, through mountains, across the sea, to the very edges of the earth. [serious] Too much. They will be like us, the gods worried. [softly] So the Heart of Sky breathed a mist onto their eyes, like breath on a mirror, so they could see only what was close. Then the gods made them wives, and they had children.

## close

[warmly] And that is why, for the Maya, maize is not just food. It is what people are made of.

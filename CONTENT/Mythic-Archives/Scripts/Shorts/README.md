## How they reach the studio

Each script is attached to its item in `maps/mythic-shorts.json` (`script`: file, title, description). On the next deploy the studio makes a held, unvoiced run for each one. Pick an outro, voice on ElevenLabs, leave the tag pass off (tags are written in). Written and attached with `scripts/attach-scripts.ts`; the brief is the `foundry-scripts` skill.

## Sources and choices: CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING

Each follows the source named on its map item, told plainly from memory of that text. Choices worth knowing:

- Pangu: people come from the creatures on his body, the version in Werner.
- Vasilisa: the skull burning the stepmother and stepsisters to ash is kept (it is the tale's ending).
- The Fifth Sun: the rabbit thrown at the moon's face, and the gods' sacrifice to set the sun moving.
- King Midas: told as Ovid tells it, with no daughter (that detail is a later retelling).
- Huitzilopochtli: the 1978 Coyolxauhqui stone found at the Templo Mayor.
- Kaggen: the moon made from his shoe after the eland is killed, from Bleek and Lloyd.
- Gawain: the green belt worn by the court afterwards, as the poem ends.

### Facts: check before publishing

- Kaguya-hime: the five tasks paraphrased; the Emperor's three years of letters; burned on the highest mountain (Fuji, not named).
- Yu: the stolen self-growing soil comes from the Shanhaijing tradition rather than the Shujing; Gun's body cut open after three years; dragon and turtle helpers; thirteen years, three doors.
- Sampo: forged from a swan feather tip, milk, a barley grain and wool (runo 10); the crane waking Pohjola; Louhi as an eagle; pieces to the sea and to Finland, the lid to Pohjola (runo 43).

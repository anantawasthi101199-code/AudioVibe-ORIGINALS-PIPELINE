## story

[warmly] This is a Russian story about a prince, a bird made of fire, and a grey wolf who was much cleverer than he was.

[curious] A tsar, the king of Russia, had a garden with a tree that grew golden apples. But every night, one of the apples disappeared. So the tsar told his three sons to keep watch. [lightly] The two older sons fell asleep. But the youngest, Ivan, stayed awake.

[with wonder] At midnight the whole garden lit up. A bird swooped down to the tree, and its feathers glowed like flames, gold and red, so bright he could barely look at it. It was the Firebird. Ivan leapt at it, and grabbed its tail. The bird pulled free and flew away, but one feather stayed in his hand, still glowing.

[determined] The tsar wanted the Firebird itself. So Ivan rode off to find it. [ominous] At a crossroads, he found a stone with words carved on it. Whoever rides straight on will lose his horse. He rode on. That night, a huge grey wolf leapt out and ate his horse.

[tender] But then the wolf spoke. I am sorry, it said. Climb on my back, and I will carry you instead.

[building tension] The wolf ran faster than the wind, and took Ivan to a garden where the Firebird sat in a golden cage. Take the bird, said the wolf, but do not touch the cage. [softly] Ivan took the bird, then thought, what a beautiful cage. He touched it. [pause] Bells rang everywhere, and the guards seized him.

## ending

[serious] That garden's king said Ivan could have the Firebird, if he first brought him a horse with a golden mane. The horse's owner sent him for Elena the Beautiful, a princess. [slyly] The wolf grabbed Elena and carried her off. But on the journey, Ivan and Elena fell in love. So the wolf took Elena's shape, and was handed over to the king in her place, then slipped away. Then it took the horse's shape, and did the same again.

[delighted] Ivan came home with Elena, the golden-maned horse, and the Firebird. [grave] His jealous brothers killed him on the road and stole it all. [tender] But the wolf found him, made a crow fetch the water of life, and brought him back. Ivan returned to his father, the brothers were punished, and he married Elena.

## close

[softly] And the grey wolf, who had once eaten his horse, said goodbye at the crossroads, and went back into the forest.

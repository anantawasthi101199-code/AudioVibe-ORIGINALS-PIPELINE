## story

[warmly] This is the story of how King Arthur got Excalibur, the most famous sword in the world, from a hand that rose out of a lake.

[curious] Arthur had only just become king of England. He was young, brave and sometimes reckless. [thoughtful] One day he heard about a knight called Pellinore, who camped beside a spring in the forest and fought every knight who rode by. Arthur decided to fight him.

[intense] They charged at each other on horseback. Their lances shattered. They jumped down and fought with swords, so fiercely that the grass around them turned red. [grave] And then, with one great blow, Arthur's sword snapped in two. Pellinore raised his own sword to finish him. [hushed] But the wizard Merlin, who was riding with Arthur, cast a spell, and Pellinore fell into a deep sleep.

[softly] Merlin took the wounded king to a hermit, a holy man living alone in the woods, who healed him over three days. Then the two of them rode on. [thoughtful] Arthur was worried. I have no sword, he said. [knowingly] Merlin smiled. There is a sword nearby, he said, and it will be yours.

[with wonder] They came to a wide, still lake. And in the middle of it, an arm rose up out of the water, dressed in a sleeve of shining white silk. In its hand it held a beautiful sword, with its blade pointing up at the sky. [pause] Then a woman came walking towards them, across the surface of the water.

## ending

[reverently] She was the Lady of the Lake. The sword is called Excalibur, she said, and it is yours, if you will give me a gift when I ask for one. Arthur promised. [lightly] So he and Merlin climbed into a little boat and rowed out across the lake. Arthur took hold of the sword by its handle, and the arm and the hand sank quietly back beneath the water.

[thoughtful] As they rode away, Merlin asked a question. Which do you like better, the sword, or its scabbard? The scabbard was the case the sword was kept in. The sword, of course, said Arthur. [serious] Then you are not wise, said Merlin. The scabbard is worth ten of the sword. As long as you wear it, you will never lose a drop of blood, however badly you are wounded.

## close

[softly] So Arthur rode home with Excalibur at his side, and the scabbard that kept him safe was the part he barely noticed.

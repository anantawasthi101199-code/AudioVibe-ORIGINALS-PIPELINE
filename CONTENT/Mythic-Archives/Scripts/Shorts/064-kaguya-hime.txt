## story

[warmly] This is a story from old Japan, about a girl who came from the moon, and the old man who found her.

[gently] There was once an old bamboo cutter who lived with his wife at the edge of a bamboo forest. Every day he went into the green stalks with his axe. [hushed] One evening he saw a stalk that was glowing, as if a lamp were burning inside it. He cut it open. And there, sitting in the hollow, was a tiny girl, no taller than his hand, shining softly.

[tender] He carried her home in his palms, and he and his wife, who had no children, raised her as their own. From that day, whenever he cut bamboo, he found gold inside the stalks, and the old couple grew rich. [with quiet awe] The girl grew too, astonishingly fast. In three months she was a young woman, so beautiful that light seemed to fill the house. They named her Kaguya-hime, the shining princess.

[curious] Men came from all over the land to see her, and five great noblemen would not give up. So she set each one a task. Bring me the Buddha's own stone begging bowl. A branch from a tree of jewels across the sea. A robe that no fire can burn. The shining jewel from a dragon's neck. A shell hidden in a swallow's nest. [knowingly] Whoever brings his thing, she said, I will marry.

[with a smile] One brought a fake bowl. One had craftsmen make a jewelled branch, and they came to the house asking to be paid. One's robe burned up. One nearly drowned hunting the dragon. One fell from the rafters reaching for the shell. Every one of them failed.

[softly] Even the Emperor came, and she refused him too, though they wrote to each other for three years. [pause] And then, every night, Kaguya-hime began to sit by the window, looking at the moon, and weeping.

## ending

[hushed] At last she told the old man. I am not from this world. I come from the moon. On the night of the next full moon, my people are coming to take me back.

[building] The Emperor sent two thousand soldiers to guard the house. At midnight, a light brighter than day came down from the sky, and the soldiers froze where they stood. Their arms would not lift.

[tender] Beings from the moon came down in a flying carriage. Kaguya-hime wrote a last letter to the Emperor, and left him a little jar, a medicine that would make him live forever. Then they wrapped her in a robe of feathers, and as it touched her, all her sorrow for the earth was gone. [softly] She stepped into the carriage, and rose into the sky.

## close

[gently] The Emperor did not want to live forever in a world without her, so he had the letter and the medicine burned on the highest mountain in Japan, the one closest to the moon, and the smoke rose from its peak long after.

## story

[warmly] This is a Japanese story about a kind young fisherman who went to a palace under the sea, and a box he was told never to open.

[softly] Urashima Taro lived in a little fishing village by the sea. He lived with his old parents, and every day he rowed out to fish. [serious] One day, walking along the beach, he saw some boys poking and kicking a small turtle they had caught. [gently] He told them to stop, and gave them some money for the turtle. Then he carried it down to the water and let it go.

[with wonder] The next day, as he fished, he heard a voice calling his name. A turtle swam up beside his boat. It was the same one. You saved my life, it said. Climb on my back, and I will take you to the Palace of the Dragon King, under the sea.

[lightly] Urashima climbed on. The turtle dived down, through blue and green water, deeper and deeper. [with wonder] And on the sea floor stood a palace of coral and pearl, glowing in the dark. Fish of every colour swam through its gates. A beautiful princess came out to greet him. The turtle had been her all along, she said, and she wanted to thank him.

[tender] Urashima stayed. Every day was a feast, with music and dancing, and he and the princess fell in love. [thoughtful] But after a while, he began to think about his old parents, who did not know where he was. [softly] I must go home, he said, just to see them. [pause] The princess was very sad. And she gave him a gift.

## ending

[serious] It was a beautiful box, lacquered and tied with a silk cord. Keep it with you, she said. But never, ever open it.

[slowly] The turtle carried him home. But the village was different. The houses were strange. Nobody knew him. He asked an old man about his family. [grave] The old man said he had heard of a fisherman called Urashima, from an old story. He had gone out to sea and never come back, three hundred years ago.

[softly] Urashima sat on the beach, lost and alone. He held the box. [intense] And without thinking, he untied the cord, and lifted the lid. [hushed] A thin white smoke drifted out, and wrapped around him. His hair turned white. His face wrinkled. In a moment, he was an old, old man.

## close

[tender] The box had been holding his three hundred years, and once it was opened, he could never go back to the sea.

## story

[warmly] This is a Russian story about a sorcerer who could not be killed, because he had hidden his own death far away, and the prince who went to find it.

[darkly] The sorcerer was Koschei the Deathless. He was old and terrible, as thin as a skeleton, and no sword, no arrow, no fire could harm him. [grave] And he had stolen the beautiful Marya, the wife of a young prince called Ivan, and carried her off to his castle.

[determined] Ivan set out to find her. Along the way, he grew so hungry he was ready to eat anything. He met a bear cub, a falcon, and then a great pike, a fish, lying stranded by a river. Each time he raised his hand to kill it for food. [tender] And each time the animal begged, spare me, and I will help you one day. So he let them go, and walked on hungry.

[thoughtful] At last he reached Koschei's castle, and found Marya alone. Together they made a plan. [slyly] That night, when Koschei came home, Marya asked him sweetly where his death was hidden, so she could keep it safe. [hushed] And the sorcerer, sure that nobody could ever reach it, told her.

[slowly] Far away, in the middle of the ocean, there is an island. On the island grows an oak tree. Under the oak tree is buried an iron chest. In the chest is a hare. In the hare is a duck. In the duck is an egg. [pause] And in the egg is a needle. And in the point of that needle is my death.

## ending

[building tension] Ivan travelled to the edge of the ocean, crossed to the island, and found the oak. But the chest was buried too deep to reach. [delighted] Then the bear came, tore up the ground, and smashed the chest open. Out leapt the hare, and raced away, but the bear caught it with one great paw. Out flew the duck, high into the sky. Down dived the falcon and struck it. [intense] Out fell the egg, into the sea. And up from the waves came the pike, holding the egg gently in its mouth.

[ominous] Far away in his castle, Koschei felt it. He grew weak, and screamed for help. [slowly] Ivan cracked the egg, took out the needle, and snapped its point.

[softly] And Koschei the Deathless fell down dead.

## close

[warmly] Ivan brought Marya home, and the animals he had once been too kind to eat had saved them both.

## story

[warmly] This is the Egyptian story of the longest court case ever held, between a young god and his uncle, over who should be king of Egypt.

[softly] Osiris, the good king of Egypt, had been murdered by his brother Set, and had gone down to rule the land of the dead. Now his throne was empty. [curious] Two gods claimed it. One was Horus, the son of Osiris, young, with the head of a falcon. The other was Set himself, god of storms, older, and much stronger.

[serious] The great gods gathered as a court to decide. Some said the crown belonged to the son. But the sun god, who led the court, liked Set. He is strong, he said. And Horus is just a boy. [wry] So they argued. And argued. For eighty years.

[slyly] Horus's mother, Isis, the cleverest of the goddesses, decided to help. She changed herself into a beautiful young widow, and found Set alone. A stranger has taken my dead husband's cattle, she told him, and thrown my son out of his home. [knowingly] Set was outraged. How can anyone give a dead man's cattle to a stranger, he said, while his son is still alive? [lightly] Isis turned into a bird, flew up into a tree, and laughed. Your own mouth has judged you, she called down.

[thoughtful] But still the court would not decide. [pause] So Set came up with contests.

## ending

[playfully] First, the two of them turned into hippopotamuses, to see who could stay under the water longer. Then Set proposed a boat race, in boats made of stone. [slyly] Horus built a boat of wood, and covered it in plaster so it looked like stone. Set broke the top off a whole mountain to make his boat. It sank at once. In a rage, Set became a hippo again and smashed Horus's boat apart.

[serious] At last, the gods wrote a letter to Osiris, down in the land of the dead, and asked what he thought. [intense] Osiris wrote back. I am the one who makes the barley grow that feeds you all, he said. And if you do not give my son his crown, I will send the demons of the dead to fetch you. [slowly] The court decided very quickly after that. Horus was crowned king of Egypt.

## close

[softly] And Set went up into the sky to ride with the sun, where his voice could still be heard, as thunder.

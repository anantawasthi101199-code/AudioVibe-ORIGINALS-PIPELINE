## story

[warmly] This is the Egyptian story of how the goddess Isis tricked the greatest god of all into giving up his secret name, and his power with it.

[curious] In ancient Egypt, a name was more than a word. Your true name held your power. Know someone's secret name, and you held power over them. [with wonder] The sun god, Ra, had many names that everybody knew. But he also had one secret name, hidden deep inside his body, which no other god had ever heard. That name was the source of all his strength.

[thoughtful] Isis was the cleverest of the gods. She knew every spell in heaven and on earth, except one. She wanted Ra's secret name.

[softly] By now Ra was very old. He was so old that, as he walked across the sky each day, he drooled, and his spit dripped down onto the earth. [slyly] Isis gathered some of it up. She mixed it with the dust it had fallen into, and with her hands she shaped it into a serpent. She breathed magic into it. Then she laid the serpent in the path that Ra walked every morning.

[building tension] Ra came walking along, with all the gods behind him. The serpent struck. It sank its fangs into him. [intense] Ra screamed so loudly his cry reached the edge of the sky. The poison burned through his body like fire. His jaws chattered. His limbs shook. [darkly] It was made from his own spit, so his own magic could not defeat it. [pause] And then Isis came to him, full of sympathy, and offered to help.

## ending

[slyly] Tell me your name, she said gently, and my spell will heal you. [thoughtful] Ra listed name after name. I am the maker of heaven and earth. I am the god of the morning, the noon and the evening. [softly] But the poison did not move, because none of those was the secret name. The pain grew worse and worse.

[slowly] At last, Ra could bear it no longer. He agreed. And his secret name passed out of his body and into hers, quietly, so that no other god could hear it. [reverently] Isis spoke her great spell, and the poison drained away. Ra was healed.

[knowingly] From that day on, Isis was the most powerful magician of all the gods, because she knew the one name that even the sun had tried to keep hidden.

## close

[hushed] And she kept it to herself, sharing it only with her own son, and the secret has never been spoken aloud since.

## story

[warmly] This is the Inca story of how their great city of Cusco, high in the mountains of Peru, was founded by the children of the Sun.

[softly] Long ago, the Incas said, people lived like wild animals. They had no houses, only caves. They wore no clothes, only leaves and animal skins. They did not know how to plant crops, and ate whatever they could find. [thoughtful] The Sun, looking down from the sky, felt sorry for them. So he decided to send down two of his own children, to teach them how to live.

[with wonder] They rose up out of Lake Titicaca, a great blue lake high in the Andes mountains. One was a son, Manco Cápac. The other was a daughter, Mama Ocllo. [reverently] The Sun gave them a staff made of solid gold, about as long as an arm. [serious] Walk wherever you like, he told them. And every time you stop to eat or sleep, push the staff into the ground. Where it sinks all the way down, in one push, that is where you will build my city.

[determined] So they set off north from the lake, walking over the mountains. Every evening, Manco pushed the golden staff into the earth. And every evening, it hit stone, and stopped. The ground was too hard. [slowly] They walked on, day after day, through cold passes and rocky valleys.

[building tension] At last they came to a wide, green valley surrounded by mountains, and climbed a hill above it. Manco lifted the golden staff, and pushed it into the soil. [pause] And it slid down, all the way, and vanished into the earth.

## ending

[warmly] This was the place. They called the city Cusco, which in their language means the navel, the centre of the world.

[gently] Then they went out to find the wild people. Manco taught the men how to plough the land, plant maize and potatoes, and dig channels to bring water from the mountains to the fields. [tender] Mama Ocllo taught the women how to spin wool, weave cloth and make clothes. People came down from the hills to learn, and they built houses, and the city grew.

[proudly] Manco Cápac became the first ruler of the Incas, and from his family came every Inca king after him, the sons of the Sun.

## close

[softly] And the city they founded, where the golden staff disappeared into the earth, still stands, high in the mountains of Peru.

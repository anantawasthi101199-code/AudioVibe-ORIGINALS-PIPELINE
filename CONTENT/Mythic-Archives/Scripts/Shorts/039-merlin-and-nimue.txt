## story

[warmly] This is the story of how Merlin, the greatest wizard in all of Britain, the man who made King Arthur a king, was trapped forever by his own magic.

[curious] Merlin could see the future. He had seen Arthur pull the sword from the stone, and the Round Table full of knights. [serious] And he had also seen his own end. One day, he told Arthur, I will be buried alive, by a woman I love. [softly] Arthur begged him to use his power to stop it. But Merlin only said that what was coming could not be changed.

[lightly] Then one of the Ladies of the Lake, a young enchantress called Nimue, came to Arthur's court. She was clever and beautiful. [tender] And Merlin, who was very old, fell in love with her.

[thoughtful] He followed her everywhere. He would not leave her alone, not for a moment. [slyly] Nimue did not love him back, and she was a little afraid of him, because he was a powerful wizard. But she was curious. So she let him stay near her, on one condition. He had to teach her his magic.

[with wonder] And he did. He taught her every spell and secret he knew. How to make a fire burn without wood. How to change shape. How to hide a castle from sight. How to bind someone so they could never break free. [softly] By the end, she knew almost as much as he did.

[ominous] They travelled together, far away, to Cornwall, in the far south-west of Britain. [pause] And there, Merlin showed her a great rock, with something hidden beneath it.

## ending

[hushed] Under the rock, he said, there is a secret chamber, full of wonders. Let me show you. [slowly] Merlin went down under the stone, into the dark. And Nimue, standing above, used the magic he had taught her.

[intense] She cast the binding spell on the rock. It closed over the opening. [darkly] Merlin called up to her, but she did not answer. All his magic could not open it, because the spell was his own. [softly] Nimue walked away, back towards Arthur's court, and left him there.

## close

[hushed] And from that day, the greatest wizard in Britain was gone, sealed beneath a stone by the spell he had taught her himself.

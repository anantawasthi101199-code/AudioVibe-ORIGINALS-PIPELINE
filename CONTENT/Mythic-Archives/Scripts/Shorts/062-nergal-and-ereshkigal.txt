## story

[warmly] This is a story from ancient Babylon, about a proud young god, the queen of the land of the dead, and a quarrel that ended in the strangest marriage of all.

[curious] Once, the great gods held a feast in heaven. Everyone was invited, except one goddess, who could never come. Ereshkigal, the queen of the dead. She ruled the great below, a dark place where the dead ate dust, and she could never leave it, not even for a day. [softly] So the gods sent her a message. Since you cannot come up to us, send your messenger to take your share of the food.

[lightly] Her messenger was a demon called Namtar. He climbed up to heaven, and as he walked into the feast, every god stood up to honour him, as the messenger of a great queen. [darkly] Every god except one. A young god of war and plague, called Nergal, stayed sitting down.

[serious] When Ereshkigal heard, she was furious. Send me the god who would not stand, she said. I will kill him.

[thoughtful] Nergal was frightened. He went to the god of wisdom for advice. [knowingly] You must go, said the wise god. But in the land of the dead, do not sit on any chair they offer. Do not eat their bread, or drink their beer. And when the queen shows you her beauty, do not give in.

[slowly] So Nergal went down, through seven gates, into the dark. [pause] And at first, he did everything right.

## ending

[building tension] He refused the chair. He refused the bread and beer. But then Ereshkigal went to bathe, and let him see her. [softly] And Nergal forgot every warning. They fell in love, and stayed together for six days and six nights.

[slyly] On the seventh day, while she slept, Nergal crept out, back up through the seven gates, and ran home to heaven.

[intense] Ereshkigal woke alone, and her heartbreak turned to rage. She sent Namtar to the gods with one message. Send him back to me. If you do not, I will raise up the dead, and they will eat the living, until the dead outnumber them.

[firmly] The gods sent Nergal back. He stormed down through the seven gates, burst into her throne room, and grabbed her by the hair. [tender] And then, instead of fighting, they held each other. Ereshkigal asked him to stay forever, and be her husband, and rule beside her.

## close

[softly] And so the god who would not stand up for death's messenger became king of the land of the dead, at the side of the queen who had wanted to kill him.

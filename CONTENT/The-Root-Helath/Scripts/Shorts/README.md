### Facts: check before publishing

- DOMS: lactate clears within about an hour; soreness peaks one to three days after; dark-brown urine as the urgent sign.
- Painkillers: ibuprofen cautions (asthma, ulcers, kidney, heart); paracetamol in pregnancy; no doses given, only 'check the leaflet'.
- Hay fever: the NHS treatments listed (antihistamines, steroid sprays, Vaseline, wraparound glasses, shower); tree and grass seasons.

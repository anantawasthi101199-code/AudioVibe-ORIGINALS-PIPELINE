## Facts: check before publishing

- Procopius one third of Italy; six thousand solidi from Anonymus Valesianus; Cassiodorus letter possibly about Romulus.
- Ibn Battuta: 55,000 dinars debt, 2,000 plus dead a day in Damascus, dates of Kilwa and Quanzhou are approximate; Rihla dates are debated.
- Ashoka: dates (coronation about 269, Kalinga about 261, death about 232 BCE), Rock Edict XIII figures, XIII absent at Dhauli and Jaugada, the kitchen line (Rock Edict I), three-day reprieve (Pillar Edict IV), Maski 1915, Prinsep 1837.
- Constantinople: Sphrantzes' count (about 4,900 Greeks and 2,000 foreigners), Ottoman army estimate, 60 oxen, ships dragged on 22 April (about 70), eclipse 22 May, Giustiniani's wound and death on Chios, Doukas on the Kerkoporta, patriarch appointed 1454.

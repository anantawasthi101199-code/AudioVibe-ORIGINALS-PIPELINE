# India True Crime - hand-written scripts

Map `india-true-crime`, format `case-in-full`, the India counterpart to `uk-true-crime`. Three series by type of case: 1 Cases That Changed India (1-8), 2 India's Most Wanted (9-16), 3 Cons, Heists and Scams (17-23). Attached with `scripts/attach-scripts.ts`; the brief is the `foundry-scripts` skill. Written from memory of the named sources, not a fetched article.

Chosen for closed cases only: every accused named was convicted, acquitted, has died, or was never tried, and no case with a live trial or a victim of sexual assault whose name Indian law protects. Keep it that way when adding cases.

### Facts: check before publishing

- Thuggee: Sleeman born Cornwall 1788; Feringeea captured and the grove dig (early 1830s, place not named); department 1835 at Jabalpur; Thuggee Act 1836 (membership alone, life imprisonment); Ramaseeana 1836; Confessions of a Thug (Meadows Taylor, 1839); Kim Wagner's conclusion as summarised; Criminal Tribes Act 1871, repealed 1949.
- Tea Garden Murder: August 1897, Jalpaiguri; victim Hriday Nath Ghosh, manager; the almanac with two marks, one a right thumb; Kangali Charan a former servant with a theft record; Calcutta bureau 1897 as the world's first; 1898 trial, theft only, two years (check); Evidence Act section 45 amended 1899; Henry to Scotland Yard 1901; 5,000 rupees each to Haque and Bose.
- Bhawal Sanyasi: three brothers' names and order; marriage 1902; death reported 8 May 1909; Buckland Bund 1920-21; sister Jyotirmoyee's recognition, declaration May 1921; suit 1930, trial from 1933, Basu judgment 24 August 1936; High Court 1940, 2-1; Privy Council 30 July 1946; death 1 August 1946 after a stroke; the "man from Punjab" claim.
- Nanavati: 27 April 1959; Jeevan Jyot, Nepean Sea Road; Metro cinema; revolver signed out on the Ahmednagar pretext; Mamie Ahuja present; Provost Marshal then police; Blitz and Russi Karanjia; the toy revolver and towel story (given as "it was said"); jury 8-1, 21 October 1959; Judge Ratilal Mehta's reference; High Court March 1960; Supreme Court November 1961; pardon 1964 by Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit; Canada, died 2003; ages 37 and about 34.
- Geeta and Sanjay Chopra: Saturday 26 August 1978; Dhaula Kuan; Yuva Vani at 8pm, father to collect at 9; Jesus and Mary College and Modern School; the witness who reported the car number and the stations passing it on; bodies found 28 August off Upper Ridge Road by a cattle grazier; arrest 8 September 1978 on the Kalka Mail in a soldiers' carriage, handed over in Delhi (check where); convictions 1979; hanged 31 January 1982 in Tihar; ICCW awards created 1978.

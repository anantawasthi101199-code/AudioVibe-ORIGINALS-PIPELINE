### Facts: check before publishing

- Great Train Robbery: 8 August 1963 just after 3am; Sears Crossing and Bridego Bridge; about 2.6m pounds, 120 bags; Leatherslade Farm about 27 miles; found 13 August; Monopoly, ketchup bottle; Aylesbury 1964, thirty-year sentences; Biggs escape 1965, return 2001; Reynolds caught 1968; about 400,000 pounds recovered; Jack Mills never drove again (check), died 1970.
- Guildford Four: 5 October 1974, five killed (names; Ann Hamilton and Caroline Slater given only as 'in their teens'); Woolwich two dead; arrests from 28 November 1974; convictions 22 October 1975 and the judge's treason remark; Giuseppe Conlon died 1980; Balcombe Street December 1975; appeal 1977; Avon and Somerset findings; quashed 19 October 1989; officers acquitted 1993; May report 1994; Blair apology 2005; Richardson died 2012, Conlon 2014.
- Pitchfork case: Lynda Mann 21 November 1983 (the Black Pad); Dawn Ashworth found 2 August 1986 (Ten Pound Lane); Buckland cleared November 1986; Jeffreys 10 September 1984; about 5,000 men screened from January 1987; the pub conversation August 1987; arrest 19 September 1987; guilty pleas January 1988; Kelly's suspended sentence; 2021 release and recall; the contaminated-tray case (not named).

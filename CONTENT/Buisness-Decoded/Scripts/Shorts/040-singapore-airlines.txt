## story

[leaning in] Most big airlines start at home, flying between cities in their own country. Singapore Airlines could not. Singapore is a single small island. There is nowhere inside it to fly to.

[warmly] Singapore sits at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula, in South-East Asia, a busy port city about the size of a large town. [curious] Until 1972, it shared an airline with its neighbour, Malaysia. Then the two countries decided to split it. Malaysia's half could fly between Malaysian cities. [serious] Singapore's half could fly only abroad. Every single flight it ever made would be in competition with the biggest and best airlines in the world.

[thoughtful] So Singapore Airlines made a decision. If it could not be the biggest, it would be the best. [determined] It bought new planes, and kept replacing them, so its aircraft were among the youngest in the sky. It trained its crew for months in how to serve, how to carry themselves, how to remember a passenger's name.

[with a smile] Its flight attendants wore a uniform made from a traditional South-East Asian dress, the sarong kebaya, designed by a French fashion house. The airline turned them into the face of its adverts, and called her the Singapore Girl. [pause] And then it set out to make Singapore itself the place the world passed through.

## rise

[building] The government built Changi Airport, which became one of the best airports in the world. Singapore Airlines connected Europe, Asia and Australia through it, so travellers changed planes in Singapore. [delighted] It was the first airline to fly the giant Airbus A380, in 2007, and it flies some of the longest routes in the world, like Singapore to New York, nonstop, for around eighteen hours. [knowingly] It worked because service was the one thing a tiny country could compete on, and it never stopped polishing it.

[quietly impressed] Today Singapore Airlines is regularly named one of the best airlines in the world.

## close

[warmly] It took a country with nowhere to fly at home, which decided to be the best at flying everywhere else.

## story

[leaning in] H&M has thousands of shops around the world. It began in 1947 with one small women's clothing shop in a quiet Swedish town, and a shopkeeper who had just come back from a trip to America.

[warmly] His name was Erling Persson. After the Second World War, he travelled around the United States, and walked through the great department stores of New York. [curious] He saw something that did not exist back home. Shops packed with clothes, sold at low prices, in enormous quantities. The idea was simple. Make each item cheap, and sell so many of them that the business still makes money.

[determined] He went home to Sweden, and in 1947 he opened a shop in the town of Västerås, a small city west of Stockholm. He called it Hennes, the Swedish word for hers, because it sold only women's clothes. [delighted] It was fashionable, it was affordable, and it was busy from the start.

[thoughtful] Over the next twenty years he opened more shops across Sweden, and then in Norway. Then, in 1968, he bought a Stockholm shop called Mauritz Widforss, which sold hunting and fishing gear and some men's clothing. [with a smile] That gave the company a new name, Hennes and Mauritz, and soon simply H and M. Now there were clothes for men and children too.

[serious] The company never owned the factories that made its clothes. It worked with factories in other countries, and kept prices low by ordering huge numbers. [pause] But to grow beyond Scandinavia, it needed something more.

## rise

[building] It needed shoppers to talk about it. And in 2004, it found the perfect way. H and M asked Karl Lagerfeld, one of the most famous fashion designers in the world, to create a collection for its shops. Designer clothes at H and M prices. [delighted] Queues formed outside shops before they opened, and in many places it sold out within an hour. [knowingly] It worked because it gave ordinary shoppers a taste of luxury, for the price of a high street T-shirt.

[quietly impressed] Big designer partnerships followed almost every year. The Persson family still controls the company, now run by later generations, and H and M is one of the largest fashion retailers on Earth.

## close

[warmly] It took one trip to America, and the confidence to sell good clothes cheaply to everyone.

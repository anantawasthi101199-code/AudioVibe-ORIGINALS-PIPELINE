## story

[leaning in] Monopoly, Nerf guns, Play-Doh and Transformers all come from one company. It started with three brothers selling scraps of leftover cloth.

[warmly] The brothers were the Hassenfelds. They had come to America from Poland, and in 1923 they started a small business in Providence, Rhode Island, on America's east coast. They called it Hassenfeld Brothers. [curious] At first, they sold remnants, the leftover ends of rolls of cloth, to other businesses. They used some of that cloth to cover pencil boxes. Soon they were making pencil cases and school supplies, and then pencils themselves.

[thoughtful] In the 1940s, they started making toys too, like doctor and nurse kits for children to play with. [delighted] And then, in 1952, a toy inventor brought them a strange idea. A set of plastic eyes, noses, mouths and ears, with spikes on the back, which children could push into a real potato from the kitchen. It was called Mr Potato Head.

[knowingly] Hasbro did something no toy company had done before. It advertised the toy on television. Children saw it and begged for it. Mr Potato Head became the first toy ever advertised on TV, and a huge hit.

[serious] In 1964, they had another problem to solve. They had made a large soldier figure with moving arms and legs, called G.I. Joe. But boys back then would not play with dolls. [pause] So Hasbro invented a new phrase.

## rise

[with a smile] It called G.I. Joe an action figure. The name stuck, and boys played with action figures happily. In 1968 the company shortened its name to Hasbro. [building] In the 1980s it launched Transformers, toy robots that change into cars and planes, with a cartoon to go with them. Then it bought famous games companies, gaining Monopoly, Scrabble and many more. [knowingly] It worked because Hasbro learned early that a toy is also a story, and television could tell that story to millions of children at once.

[quietly impressed] Today Hasbro is one of the biggest toy and game companies in the world.

## close

[warmly] It took three brothers who started with scraps, and a willingness to try what no toy company had tried before.
